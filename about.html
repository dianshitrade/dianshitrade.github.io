About

Card Cosmic helps gift card sellers make clearer decisions.

We built this site to explain rates, supported card categories and app download options for Nigerian gift card traders.

Our role

Card Cosmic is not designed as a public chat-only landing page. It is an app-first experience supported by education, transparent category pages and direct support options.

This website helps users understand the process before they begin: what card information matters, why rates change, and where to download the app.

Company information

Card Cosmic is operated by Guangxi Dianshi Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd.

What we focus on

  • Clear daily rate guidance
  • Useful gift card education
  • App-first trading journey
  • Accessible support channel
  • Transparent company details
