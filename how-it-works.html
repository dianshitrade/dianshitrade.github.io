How to sell gift cards with Card Cosmic.

The website helps you learn and check categories. The app and support channel help you continue when you are ready to trade.

Prepare your card

Know the brand, country, balance and whether you have a clear receipt or proof of purchase.

Check rate guidance

Review the Rates page, then confirm the latest offer because demand and card country can affect pricing.

Use the app

Download Card Cosmic from the App Store or Google Play and follow the app-first trade flow.

Contact support

Use WhatsApp if you have questions about card category, rate, app download or supported countries.

Follow review steps

Submit the requested information clearly so your card can be reviewed without unnecessary delays.

Receive payout guidance

Card Cosmic focuses on Naira payout for Nigerian traders. Payment timing depends on successful review.

Start with rate clarity.

Check popular rates, download the app, or ask support for today’s guidance.
