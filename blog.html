Gift Card Basics

What is a gift card?

A gift card is stored value issued by a brand or payment network. It can be a physical card or a digital code used to shop, game, subscribe or send value.

Functions

What can gift cards do?

Gift cards help people buy products, fund game wallets, pay for digital services, give presents, or convert unused card value through trusted trading platforms.

Nigeria

Why are they useful in Nigeria?

Many Nigerian users receive foreign gift cards, use them for online services, or sell supported cards for Naira when they need local spending value.
