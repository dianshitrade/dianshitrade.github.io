What affects your rate?

Gift card rates depend on card brand, country, balance, receipt availability and market demand. A US Apple card may not carry the same rate as a UK Apple card, and rates can move during the day.

For the most accurate offer, share the card type, country and value in the Card Cosmic app or message support on WhatsApp.
