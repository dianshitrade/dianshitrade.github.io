Popular cards Nigerians sell

Apple, Steam, Razer Gold, Xbox, Amazon, Google Play, Sephora and Visa gift cards are common categories. Each card type can have different demand, and the country of the card often matters as much as the brand.

Why use an app-first flow?

An app-first process keeps rate checking, card review and support more organised than scattered chat-only trading. WhatsApp remains useful for questions and guidance before you start.

Before you trade, prepare:

  • Gift card brand and country
  • Card value and balance
  • Clear card image if requested
  • Receipt or source proof where available
  • Your preferred Naira payout details inside the app flow
