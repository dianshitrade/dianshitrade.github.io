Where do I enter 000000?

Copy the six-digit code on this page. Open CardCosmic, start creating your account, and enter 000000 when the app asks for an invitation or referral code. Check it before completing registration. If you cannot find the field, ask in-app support before finishing signup.

Does downloading automatically apply my code?

No. Opening an app store or copying the code is not registration. You need to enter 000000 in the app during signup. This website cannot confirm whether a code was entered in your app account.

Which cards can I sell, and at what rate?

Check the CardCosmic app for supported brands, countries, denominations and current quotes. Rates and availability can change. A card must meet the app's verification requirements before a trade can be completed.

When can I withdraw the ₦3,000 reward?

Register with invitation code 000000, then successfully complete your first eligible gift-card trade. The ₦3,000 cash reward is unlocked for withdrawal after that first successful trade, not after registration alone. Submitting a card is not the same as completing a trade. Failed, rejected or pending trades do not qualify. Check the current campaign eligibility, reward crediting status, identity checks, withdrawal limits and fees in your account before requesting payment. This promotional page does not issue rewards.

Is CardCosmic legit? What can I check?

Review the App Store listing and reviews or the Google Play listing, read the in-app terms, and contact support before trading. The workflow above shows CardCosmic's product screens, not independent proof of payout. Platform statistics and website testimonials are supplied by CardCosmic. No platform can eliminate every trading risk; keep your trade on-platform.

How does the 1% referral bonus work?

After registration, share your own personal code from the Friends section. Qualifying successful trades by your referred friends can earn you a 1% bonus. The Referral Program Rules in your account define the calculation base, eligibility, crediting and withdrawal conditions. This page does not promise a lifetime or unconditional payment.

Does a 30-second check mean an instant payout?

No. CardCosmic reports initial SI checks within 30 seconds for supported cards. Verification exceptions, completing the trade and processing a withdrawal are separate steps and can take longer.

Can I register without downloading the app?

New accounts currently need to complete registration in the CardCosmic mobile app. The web app is available, but its Sign Up page directs new users to download the app. Enter 000000 in the app's invitation code field before completing signup.

Need help with registration or a trade?

Contact 24/7 support inside CardCosmic. Do not share your password, OTP or unredeemed gift card code in public comments, and do not move a trade to a private off-platform deal.
